# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), deployed to Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Primary: local women in Fortaleza (CE, Brazil) seeking salon/spa beauty services — hair, face & brows, bridal, and urban spa — who want to browse services, see results, and book appointments. Secondary: returning clients rebooking, and gift/event shoppers planning bridal packages.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and conversion landing page for BELLA Salon & Spa: present the brand, its services and units, build trust through social proof, and drive a single, clear action — booking an appointment.

## Positioning

Urban spa and premium women's beauty salon combining technical sophistication with warm, welcoming care. Brand voice: empathetic, welcoming, elegant, aspirational, valuing self-esteem and the sensory experience of personal care. Hero concept: "Você já se sentiu Bella hoje?" ("Have you felt Bella today?").

## Product Principles

1. Conversion-first but unhurried: every section earns the next scroll and funnels to a single booking action.
2. Local trust: surface real location, hours, and contact details prominently; never fabricate social proof.
3. Premium yet warm: avoid clinical minimalism; lean into the provided earthy/champagne palette and serif editorial tone.
4. Mobile-first: most discovery and booking will happen on phones; the sticky booking CTA must remain reachable.
5. Content-ready: layouts must accommodate real assets when they arrive without breaking rhythm.
